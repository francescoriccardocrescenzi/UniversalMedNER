\begin{frame}{Method}
  Two-stage LoRA fine-tuning on top of MedGemma:
  \begin{enumerate}
    \item \textbf{Supervised Fine-Tuning (SFT)} \\
      LoRA adapters trained on instruction-formatted NER examples (Hu et al., 2021).
    \item \textbf{GRPO Reinforcement Learning} \\
      LoRA adapters further trained with Group Relative Policy Optimization on top of the SFT checkpoint (Shao et al., 2024).
  \end{enumerate}
  \vfill
  \begin{block}{Why LoRA?}
    Parameter-efficient adaptation of a large model without full fine-tuning cost.
  \end{block}
\end{frame}
